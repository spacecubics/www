Space Cubics hosted [Zephyr Project Meetup; Sapporo, Japan 2026][1] on
Friday, June 26, 2026.

The [Zephyr Project][2] develops Zephyr, an open-source real-time operating
system (RTOS) for IoT devices and embedded systems. Engineers, researchers,
and developers from across Japan gathered at the meetup to share their
experience and discuss recent technologies and use cases.

The event included technical sessions, lightning talks, and lively
discussions during networking. Thank you to everyone who took part.

### Event recap (added later)

The Zephyr Project published an [event recap][3] on August 27, 2026.
The blog covers the presentations and community discussions, with photos
from the venue. Please take a look to revisit the talks or get a sense of
the event.

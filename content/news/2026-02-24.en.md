The launch date for the 3U Cubesat "SC-Sat1a," developed by Space Cubics,
has been confirmed for February 25, 2026, or later.

The satellite will be carried by Space One’s "KAIROS Flight 3" and is
scheduled to be launched from Spaceport Kii in Wakayama Prefecture during
 the launch window starting February 25, 2026.

### Satellite Mission
In-orbit demonstration of the "SC-OBC Module A1," an on-board computer
module designed for small satellites.

### Launch Overview
* Rocket: KAIROS Flight 3
* Launch Window: From February 25, 2026
* Launch Site: Spaceport Kii
* Satellite Information: [SC-Sat1a Introduction](https://forum.spacecubics.com/t/topic/111) (Japanese)

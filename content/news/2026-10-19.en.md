Space Cubics will exhibit at the [70th Space Sciences and Technology
Conference][1] at Light Cube Utsunomiya, Japan, from November 10 to 13, 2026.
Our CEO, Yasushi Shoji, will also give a presentation during the conference.
We look forward to seeing you there.

### Space Cubics booth (C-01)

We will display the [SC-OBC Module V1][2], our high-end computer module for
New Space applications. We also plan to exhibit the [SC-OBC Module A1][3]
computer module for ultra-small satellites and the SC-OBC Module A1
Development Kit.

#### Products on display

- [SC-OBC Module V1][2]
- [SC-OBC Module A1][3]
- SC-OBC Module A1 Development Kit

### Presentation

The title below is an English translation of the Japanese presentation title.

- Title: 2L16 Commercial OBC development and the use of open-source software
  in platform software: Experience with SC-OBC V1 and future prospects
- Speaker: Yasushi Shoji, CEO
- Session: November 11, 2026, 15:15 to 16:30 JST (the presentation is planned
  to last approximately 10 minutes within this session)
- Venue: Room L (Small Meeting Room 401, 4th floor)

See the [official program][4] for details.

### Meetings during the conference

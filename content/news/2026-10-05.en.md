Space Cubics will exhibit at the [39th Microelectronics Workshop
(MEWS39)][1] on October 22 and 23, 2026, at the
[Tsukuba International Congress Center][2] in Japan.

Organized by JAXA, MEWS is Japan's only international workshop dedicated
to space parts and component technologies. Companies and research
institutions from Japan and abroad gather to discuss the latest
technologies and research results.

We will display our onboard computers (OBCs) at booth 40 in the exhibition
area. Please stop by our booth if you are attending.

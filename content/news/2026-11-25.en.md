Three members of our team will be speaking at [Open Source Summit Japan 2026][1], taking place over three days from Monday, December 7 to Wednesday, December 9, 2026.
This conference is a global event bringing together the latest expertise in Zephyr RTOS and embedded technologies.
A total of four session proposals submitted by our company have been accepted.
Our presentations will cover practical use cases of [Zephyr][2] in real-world spacecraft development environments, as well as verification methodologies to enhance reliability and safety.

We look forward to seeing you at the [Toranomon Hills Forum][7] (or online) during the event.

### Session Overview

#### 1. Yasushi Shoji

- **Session 1**
  - **Title:** [SEE Fault Injection and Mitigation Testing With Zephyr][3]
  - **Date & Time:** Tuesday, December 8, 2026, 14:50 – 15:30
  - **Location:** Hall B3 (4F)
- **Session 2**
  - **Title:** [From Documentation to Detectors: Making Zephyr API Contracts Machine-Checkable][4]
  - **Date & Time:** Wednesday, December 9, 2026, 10:50 – 11:30
  - **Location:** Hall B3 (4F)

#### 2. Takuya Sasaki
- **Title:** [Zephyr OTA Updates in Practice: Lessons Learned From Spacecraft Development][5]
- **Date & Time:** Tuesday, December 8, 2026, 16:40 – 17:20
- **Location:** Hall B3 (4F)

#### 3. Takumi Ando
- **Title:** [Where Should Zephyr's RAM Live? TCM, DMA, OCM, and TF-A on AMD Versal][6]
- **Date & Time:** Wednesday, December 9, 2026, 11:40 – 12:20
- **Location:** Hall B3 (4F)

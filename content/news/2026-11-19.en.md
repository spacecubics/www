We will be exhibiting at the Hokkaido Booth at [NIHONBASHI SPACE WEEK 2026][1], held over three days from Tuesday, December 1 to Thursday, December 3, 2026, in the Nihonbashi area of Tokyo.

NIHONBASHI SPACE WEEK is one of Asia's largest space business events, organized by [Cross U][2].

At the venue, we will display our space-grade OBC.

If you are attending the event, please be sure to stop by the Hokkaido Booth.

### Exhibition Overview

- **Dates:** December 1 (Tue) – December 3 (Thu), 2026
- **Venue:** Nihonbashi Mitsui Hall
- **Booth:** Hokkaido Booth (Booth No. **1-07**)
- **Exhibit:** Space-grade OBC
